# HomeLedger

A digital record of everything important about your house.

- Register your home
- Add appliances and equipment
- Record installation dates and warranties
- Upload receipts and contractor invoices
- Track maintenance and repair history
- See upcoming maintenance tasks

Single-file app (`index.html`), no build step. Open it in a browser. Data is stored locally in the browser (localStorage and IndexedDB); use My home > Export backup to keep a copy.
